Four-Pillar Case Audit

AI-Assisted Research · Human-in-the-Loop Scholarship

The Four Pillars

Reference

🛡 Ethical

Can I responsibly use these data?

Protecting participant data and intellectual property; preventing confidential data leakage.

✓ Valid

How will I verify AI’s output?

Adversarial review of outputs; treating AI agreement as potential sycophancy, not validation.

↻ Reproducible

Could another researcher understand exactly how AI was used?

Documenting specific prompts, model versions, and generation parameters.

👁 Transparent

Would I need to disclose this AI use?

Disclosing AI use precisely in manuscripts and methodologies.

Ground rule: you are auditing a workflow, not a person. Assume good intent and ordinary time pressure. The question is not “was she careless?” but “at what point did this stop being defensible, and what would have made it defensible?”

The Case

Case A

Part 1 — Name the Core Problem

3 min

Before auditing pillar by pillar, state the single most serious problem in one sentence. You will revisit this at the end to see whether the audit changed your mind.

Part 2 — Audit Each Pillar

9 min

For each pillar: what actually went wrong, what should have happened instead, and how serious the failure is. Rate severity even when you disagree within the group — the disagreement is the useful part.

Part 3 — The Point of No Return

3 min

Each decision below is individually reasonable, which is exactly why researchers make them. Select the one decision after which the workflow could no longer be defended, even with everything that followed done well.

Part 4 — Redesign the Workflow

4 min

AI is not prohibited here. Rebuild this study so the same analytic help would be defensible. Specify what AI does, what the human decides, how the output is verified, and what gets recorded.

Part 5 — Draft the Disclosure

3 min

Write the AI-use statement this study should have carried. Two or three sentences. Use the checklist to test whether yours is complete.

Part 6 — Report Out

Debrief
A workflow can produce an accurate result and still fail the audit. Accuracy is one pillar of four.
